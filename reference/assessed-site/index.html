How to Change Your Discord Profile Picture

On Desktop

  1. Open Discord and click the gear icon next to your username to open User Settings .
  2. Click on My Account from the left side menu.
  3. Click the circular Edit icon on your current avatar.
  4. Choose a new image file from your computer.
  5. Click Save to update your profile picture.

On Mobile

  1. Launch the Discord app and tap your profile icon in the bottom right corner.
  2. Tap the Edit icon to update your profile.
  3. Tap your avatar to upload a new image from your gallery or take a photo.
  4. Tap Save to apply the changes.
